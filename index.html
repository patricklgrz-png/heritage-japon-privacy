Privacy Policy

Heritage Japon Content Manager · Effective October 25, 2026

Heritage Japon Content Manager is a private, internal tool used by Heritage Japon to organize content for its own Pinterest business account and its Etsy shop. It is not a public consumer application.

1. Who operates this application

The application is operated by Heritage Japon for its own business use. For privacy questions or requests, contact us through our Etsy shop.

2. Information accessed

With authorization from the account owner, the application may access the following information:

  • Identifiers and basic account information for Heritage Japon's Pinterest account.
  • Pinterest boards, Pins, their identifiers, titles, descriptions, destination links, and related metadata.
  • Heritage Japon's own Etsy listing identifiers, product information, availability status, and product photographs used to prepare content.
  • Technical records needed to track synchronization, prevent duplicate Pins, and diagnose errors.

The application does not need Pinterest or Etsy account passwords, and it is not designed to collect Etsy customers' order information, messages, or payment details.

3. Purposes of use

Information is used to identify previously published Pins, avoid duplicate publications, organize product photographs, and prepare Pinterest content for Heritage Japon's own products. If publishing is enabled in the future, Pins will be created only following approval by the operator.

4. Storage and security

Operational data is stored locally on the operator's computer. OAuth credentials and tokens are intended to be stored in the macOS Keychain. Access is restricted to the authorized operator. No security method guarantees absolute protection.

5. Sharing and third-party services

The application communicates with Pinterest and Etsy as needed for authorized functions. When a Pin is approved for publication, the approved image, text, and destination link may be sent to Pinterest. Heritage Japon does not sell the data accessed through this application or use it to build advertising profiles of other Pinterest users.

6. Retention, deletion, and revocation

Local records are kept only while they are needed to operate the tool or to prevent duplicate publications, and may be removed by the operator. Revoking Pinterest access stops future authorized retrieval; it does not automatically erase data already stored locally. To request deletion or ask about the data, contact Heritage Japon through the Etsy shop link above. Pinterest permissions can be revoked from the connected-app settings in the Pinterest account.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
