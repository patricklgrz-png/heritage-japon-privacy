Privacy Policy

Heritage Japon Content Manager
Last updated: October 25, 2026

Heritage Japon Content Manager is a private, internal application developed for Heritage Japon to manage content associated with its own Pinterest business account and Etsy shop.

This application is intended exclusively for Heritage Japon's internal business operations. It is not offered as a public consumer service.

1. Application operator

Heritage Japon operates Heritage Japon Content Manager for its own business purposes.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through our official Etsy shop: Heritage Japon on Etsy .

2. Information accessed

With authorization from the Pinterest account owner, the application is designed to access:

  • Basic information about Heritage Japon's authorized Pinterest account.
  • Accessible Pinterest boards, including identifiers, names, visibility settings and Pin counts.
  • Accessible Pins, including identifiers, associated boards, titles, destination links and publication dates.
  • Heritage Japon's own Etsy product listings, product availability and photographs.
  • Application settings manually entered by the authorized operator.

Pinterest access is limited to the permissions required for the application's functionality: boards:read, pins:read and user_accounts:read.

The application does not collect Pinterest passwords, Etsy customer orders, customer messages or payment details.

3. Purpose of processing

The application is designed to:

  • Inspect existing Pinterest boards and Pins.
  • Identify potential duplicate publications.
  • Organize Heritage Japon's product photographs.
  • Prepare Pinterest content linked to Heritage Japon's Etsy listings.
  • Support human review and approval before future publications.

The Pinterest integration is currently under development. Its initial functionality is read-only. It does not currently create, modify or delete Pins through the Pinterest API.

If publishing functionality is introduced later, it will require explicit approval from the authorized operator, and this policy will be reviewed before activation.

4. Data storage and security

The application is designed to process information retrieved from the Pinterest API temporarily in the computer's working memory.

Pinterest account information, board metadata, Pin identifiers and retrieved Pin inventories are not intended to be permanently stored in local databases, caches, log files or backups.

When Pinterest information is required, the application retrieves it through the authorized API rather than relying on a stored copy.

Heritage Japon's own Etsy product information, locally held photographs and operator-entered application settings may be stored locally for the operation of the tool.

Pinterest application credentials and OAuth tokens are stored securely in the macOS Keychain. They are not published on this website or included in the application's source code.

Access to the application is restricted to the authorized operator. No technical security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

5. Data sharing and third-party services

The application communicates with Pinterest and Etsy only as necessary to perform authorized functions.

Heritage Japon does not sell Pinterest API data, disclose it to advertising services or use it to create advertising profiles of other users.

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical information when visitors access this page, according to its own privacy statement.

6. Data retention and deletion

Under the intended application design, information retrieved through the Pinterest API is processed temporarily and discarded after the relevant operation.

No persistent Pinterest Pin inventory is maintained by the application.

Heritage Japon's locally managed Etsy data, product photographs and application settings may be deleted by the authorized operator when no longer required.

7. Authorization and revocation

Access to Pinterest requires authorization through Pinterest's OAuth process.

The account owner may revoke the application's access through Pinterest's connected-app settings.

OAuth credentials and tokens stored in the macOS Keychain may also be deleted locally. Revocation prevents further authorized access through the revoked credentials.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Heritage Japon through our Etsy shop .

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality, permissions or data processing practices change.

The date displayed at the top of this document will reflect the latest update.
